Too many screenshots on iPhone? Sort, search and clear them

Screenshots are the to-do list nobody planned: a delivery code, a concert ticket, a recipe, an address someone sent, an error message for IT. They pile up because each one was useful for a minute, and then they bury the photos you actually care about. The fix is not deleting faster. It is sorting by what each screenshot is for, acting on the few that still matter, and clearing the rest in batches.

First, sort by job, not by date

A camera roll sorts screenshots by date, which is exactly why they are hard to find. What you remember is what the screenshot was for. Group them that way and the decision for each group becomes obvious:

GroupTypical screenshotsWhat usually happens
Receipts, ShoppingOrder confirmations, totals, return labelsKeep until the return window closes, then clear
Travel, TicketsBoarding passes, bookings, event ticketsAct before the date, clear after it
Maps & PlacesAddresses and pins people sent youOpen in Maps once, then clear
Chats & SocialConversations you wanted to rememberKeep a few, clear most
Work, StudySlides, notes, error messagesCopy the text out, then clear
Recipes & Food, InspirationIdeas for laterKeep the ones you will really use
SensitiveAnything with personal detailsLock or delete; never leave it lying in the camera roll

Clearing safely

ShotInbox never auto-deletes. When you clear screenshots, the Photos app asks for a final confirmation, and ShotInbox itself cannot undo a deletion, so treat that confirmation as the real decision. A good rhythm: act on the screenshots that still matter (add the event to your calendar, open the address in Maps, copy the order number), then clear the group in one go.

For the Sensitive group, deleting is only half the job. If a screenshot holds personal details you still need, keeping it behind a lock is safer than leaving it in the camera roll.
