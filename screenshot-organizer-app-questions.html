What ShotInbox does

  • Sorts automatically: Shopping, Receipts, Travel, Tickets, Maps & Places, Chats & Social, Work, Study, Recipes & Food, Inspiration, QR & Codes, Errors & Tech, Sensitive.
  • Searches the text inside any screenshot.
  • One-tap actions: Open in Maps, Open Link, Copy Text, Open Code, Call Number, Track Package, Add Reminder, Add to Calendar.
  • On-device: Apple Vision and Natural Language run on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

Questions

I screenshot order confirmations and tracking numbers. How does ShotInbox help?

Tracking numbers are detected, so you can track a package in one tap, and those screenshots land in Shopping or Receipts where you can find them again.

Can I find a screenshot by a word I remember from it?

Yes. Search looks at the text inside every screenshot, not just the date it was taken.

Can a screenshot of an event or appointment become a calendar entry?

Yes. Detected events and reminders are listed in time order, and you can add a reminder or add the event to Calendar in one tap.

What about screenshots with sensitive information?

ShotInbox has its own Sensitive category. Recognition runs on your device with Apple Vision and Natural Language, and nothing is uploaded.

Will it delete my screenshots on its own?

No. You choose which screenshots you are done with and clear them to reclaim storage. Nothing is ever deleted automatically, and Photos asks you to confirm first.

Who is ShotInbox for?

For anyone whose camera roll is full of receipts, tickets, addresses and codes they can never find again. ShotInbox sorts them on your iPhone and turns them into one-tap actions. Lifetime access is one purchase, with no subscription.
