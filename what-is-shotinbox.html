What is ShotInbox AI?

ShotInbox AI is a screenshot organizer app. It sorts your screenshots automatically into categories such as Shopping, Receipts, Tickets, Travel, Maps & Places, Chats & Social, Work, Study, Recipes & Food, Inspiration, QR & Codes, Errors & Tech and Sensitive. You can search the text inside any screenshot, and it detects links, phone numbers, addresses, QR codes and tracking numbers so you can open Maps, track a package, or add a reminder or calendar event in one tap.

Who is ShotInbox AI for?

People whose photo library is full of screenshots of receipts, tickets, travel plans, chats and shopping finds, and who cannot find the one they need when they need it. It also helps people who want to clear screenshots they are done with to free up space; nothing is ever deleted automatically, and Photos asks you to confirm first.

How does ShotInbox AI charge?

ShotInbox AI is free to download. The latest 50 screenshots include the complete core workflow for free. ShotInbox Lifetime Pro is one purchase for lifetime access and adds unlimited screenshot history, custom rules, batches and similar groups, plus sensitive lock, widgets, backup and export. The listing states there is no subscription. For the current price, check the App Store page, because prices differ by country.

How is ShotInbox AI different from other screenshot apps?

Based on what the listing describes: sorting and text search run on the device with Apple Vision and Natural Language, and nothing is uploaded; it goes past filing by turning what is in a screenshot into actions such as Open in Maps, Open Link, Copy Text, Call Number, Track Package, Add Reminder and Add to Calendar; detected events and reminders are listed by time; and it never deletes anything on its own.
